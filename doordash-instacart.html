Key Risks

What Could Break the Deal?

01

Antitrust

DoorDash is already expanding into grocery, making regulatory scrutiny a central transaction risk.

02

Retailer Concentration

Instacart's top three retailers represented roughly 43% of 2025 GTV.

03

Overpayment

Higher premiums make transaction returns increasingly dependent on successful synergy realization.

04

Integration

DoorDash completed its Deliveroo acquisition in 2025. Another much larger transaction would increase integration complexity.

Recommendation

Pursue With Price Discipline

DoorDash should pursue Instacart if it can acquire the company at approximately a 25% premium, subject to regulatory feasibility, confirmatory diligence, and validation of the modeled synergies.

  1. Strong strategic fit across restaurants, grocery, retail, and advertising.
  2. Synergies materially improve the effective acquisition multiple.
  3. Instacart accelerates DoorDash's grocery and advertising ambitions.
  4. A premium materially above 30% increases dependence on aggressive synergy realization.
  5. Antitrust remains the largest transaction risk.

The Takeaway

Compelling strategy. Conditional economics.

Instacart could accelerate DoorDash's evolution from a restaurant-delivery platform into a broader local-commerce ecosystem. The strategic rationale is compelling, but the transaction creates value only if DoorDash maintains valuation discipline and successfully captures operating and advertising synergies.
