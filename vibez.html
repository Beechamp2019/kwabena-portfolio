01 · The idea

Campus feels smaller when everyone can find their people.

University life is full of conversations, interests, opportunities, and shared moments, but they are often scattered across group chats and platforms that were not designed around a campus.

I built Vibez as one student-centered space for campus life. Verified members can move between the wider feed, focused communities, private conversations, and casual games without losing the context of who the product is for.

01

02 · Product experience

One campus, with room for every side of student life.

Vibez combines broad campus discovery with smaller communities and mobile-first interactions, so students can choose how public, focused, or playful they want each visit to be.

03 · Business plan

The business plan.

  1. Capture the Ghanaian university market by filling the gap for campus-focused social platforms that have proven popular among students elsewhere.
  2. Build strong campus communities through conversations, communities, social games, events, and student-driven content.
  3. Monetize through advertising, promoted events, sponsored content, and partnerships with student organizations and campus-focused businesses.
  4. Scale across universities in Ghana, expand into other African markets, then evaluate broader international growth.

04 · Building it

A social product with campus boundaries built into the system.

Vibez uses Vite and TypeScript for a responsive web application, Python for supporting application workflows, and Capacitor to package the same product for iOS and Android. The interface adapts from a desktop sidebar to a five-tab mobile navigation designed around everyday use.

Supabase supports verified email authentication, campus-scoped Postgres data, private messaging, realtime activity, storage, and Edge Functions. Server-side authorization keeps communities separated while moderation, blocking, reporting, and account controls support safer participation.

FrontendVite + TypeScript

A responsive interface spanning feeds, communities, chat, games, and profiles.

BackendPython + Supabase

Supporting workflows, verified authentication, campus-scoped Postgres data, storage, realtime, and Edge Functions.

MobileCapacitor

A shared web product packaged for iOS and Android with native push notifications.

TrustSafety systems

Moderation, reporting, blocking, privacy controls, and complete account deletion.
