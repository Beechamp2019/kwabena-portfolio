Olist Marketplace Analytics

What Drives Marketplace Revenue and Repeat Purchasing?

I built a PostgreSQL database from real Olist e-commerce data and used SQL to analyze marketplace revenue, customer retention, category performance, and geographic concentration.

Analysis Summary

Key Findings

Revenue Growth

Monthly product revenue expanded substantially through 2017 and remained near R$0.8M–R$1.0M during much of 2018.

Customer Retention

Only 3.0% of customers placed multiple delivered orders during the observed period.

Category Economics

Watches & Gifts generated high marketplace revenue despite lower order volume because revenue per order substantially exceeded several larger categories.

SQL Workflow

From Raw Tables to Business Insight

WITH customer_orders AS (
    SELECT
        c.customer_unique_id,
        COUNT(DISTINCT o.order_id) AS delivered_orders
    FROM customers c
    JOIN orders o
        ON o.customer_id = c.customer_id
    WHERE o.order_status = 'delivered'
    GROUP BY c.customer_unique_id
)
SELECT
    COUNT(*) AS unique_customers,
    COUNT(*) FILTER (
        WHERE delivered_orders > 1
    ) AS repeat_customers,
    ROUND(
        100.0 * COUNT(*) FILTER (
            WHERE delivered_orders > 1
        ) / COUNT(*),
        2
    ) AS repeat_customer_rate_pct
FROM customer_orders;
